Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Simply Split LLC collects, uses, and protects information related to the Simply Split mobile app.

Simply Split may collect and store information needed to operate the app, including account information, profile information, workout-related data, and other information you choose to provide.

How we use information

We use information to provide app functionality, manage user accounts, support authentication, save workout data, improve app reliability, respond to support requests, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Data sharing

Simply Split does not sell personal data. We may share limited information with service providers only as necessary to operate the app, such as authentication, cloud storage, support, security, analytics, or legal compliance.

Account and data deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data through the in-app deletion feature, if available, or by contacting us at simplysplit.dev@gmail.com.

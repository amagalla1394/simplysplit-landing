Account & Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Simply Split account and associated data by using the in-app deletion feature, if available, or by contacting us at:

simplysplit.dev@gmail.com

What may be deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, Simply Split LLC may delete or anonymize account information, profile information, workout-related data associated with the account, and other user-submitted information reasonably linked to the account.

Possible retention

Some information may be retained for a limited time where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backup and recovery processes, or enforcement of our rights.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot access your account or use any in-app deletion feature, contact simplysplit.dev@gmail.com and provide enough information for us to identify your account.

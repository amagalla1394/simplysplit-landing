About Us

Simply Split was founded on one simple belief: workout tracking should be simple.

In a fitness app market increasingly filled with social features, dietary tracking, and unnecessary complexity, we saw a need for something different. We wanted to create an application that stays true to its purpose—helping people organize their workouts, track their training, and monitor their progress without distractions.

Simply Split is designed to offer a straightforward yet interactive experience. We believe simplicity shouldn't mean sacrificing functionality, and that tracking your fitness journey should feel engaging rather than static or overwhelming.

We're not trying to become another social media platform or provide nutritional guidance outside our expertise. Our focus is on doing one thing well: making workout tracking simple, useful, and accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make workout tracking simple, engaging, and accessible to everyone. We believe fitness technology should empower individuals, not overwhelm them with unnecessary complexity, distractions, or financial barriers.

At Simply Split, we're committed to keeping our core workout tracking experience free for everyone. As we continue to grow and introduce new capabilities, our focus will remain on providing meaningful features that enhance the user experience without compromising the simplicity and accessibility that inspired us.

Our belief is simple: tracking your progress should never require a subscription.

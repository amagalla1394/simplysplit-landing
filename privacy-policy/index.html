Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Simply Split LLC collects, uses, shares, protects, and retains information related to the Simply Split mobile app.

About Simply Split

Simply Split is a mobile fitness app that allows users to create workout splits, log workouts, manage profile information, and view workout history.

Information we may collect

Simply Split may collect information provided directly by users during account creation, profile setup, workout entry, and in-app activity.

This may include account information, profile information, workout-related data, and other information you choose to provide while using the app.

We may also process device or app usage data needed for app functionality, reliability, and security. Some data may be processed through service providers, such as Supabase, for authentication and data storage.

How we use information

We use information to create and manage user accounts, provide app functionality, save workout and profile data, maintain account security, respond to support or technical issues, and support the ongoing operation and improvement of the app.

We may process information where it is needed to provide the app, where it supports legitimate business interests, where you have given consent, or where required by law.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Simply Split may share limited personal data with service providers only as necessary to operate the app, such as for authentication, cloud storage, support, security, app functionality, or legal compliance.

Data retention

Simply Split may retain personal data when there is an ongoing legitimate business need to do so.

When there is no longer a need to retain personal data, Simply Split may delete or aggregate it. If deletion is not immediately possible, the data may be securely stored and isolated from further processing until deletion is possible.

Security

Simply Split uses appropriate technical and organizational measures to help protect the personal data it collects and processes. These measures are designed to provide a level of security appropriate to the risk of processing the data.

If you have concerns about the security of your information, please contact us.

Account and data deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated account data through any available in-app deletion feature or by contacting Simply Split LLC.

Some information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backup and recovery, dispute resolution, enforcement of our rights, or other purposes allowed by law.

Changes to this policy

Simply Split LLC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes may be communicated through the app, website, email, or other appropriate notice.

Mobile workout tracking

Build your split. Log your workouts. Track your progress.

Simply Split helps you create structured workout splits, track sets, reps, and weight, and keep a clear history of your completed training sessions.

What it does

A simple way to manage structured training

Create workout splits

Build custom split days like Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, or your own routine.

Track each workout

Log exercises, sets, reps, and weight during planned or free training sessions.

View workout history

Keep a record of completed sessions so you can look back at what you trained.

Stay in control

Manage your profile, app preferences, account access, and data deletion options.

Built for

Gym-goers who want structure without clutter

Simply Split is designed for people who already follow a workout plan or want a cleaner way to organize one. It focuses on the basics: split days, workout logging, history, and account control.
